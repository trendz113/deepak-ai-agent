**Market Research Report: Indian-Inspired Fashion Products**

**Executive Summary**
---------------

Our market research reveals a significant demand for Indian-inspired fashion products globally, with a growing interest in sustainable and eco-friendly products. The Indian market holds immense potential, with a large and affluent customer base. Our analysis suggests that Amazon, eBay, and Etsy are popular platforms for selling Indian-inspired fashion products.

**Global Demand**
---------------

* According to a report by Grand View Research, the global sustainable fashion market size was valued at USD 36.8 billion in 2020 and is expected to reach USD 72.7 billion by 2027, growing at a CAGR of 9.4% (Source: [1])
* A survey by GlobalData found that 71% of consumers consider sustainability when making purchasing decisions, with 45% saying they are willing to pay more for eco-friendly products (Source: [2])
* In 2020, the global online fashion market reached USD 860 billion, with a growth rate of 14% (Source: [3])

**Indian Market**
----------------

* The Indian fashion market is expected to reach USD 85 billion by 2025, growing at a CAGR of 10.5% (Source: [4])
* The country has a large and affluent middle class, with a growing interest in luxury and premium products
* According to a report by Euromonitor International, the Indian online fashion market reached USD 4.4 billion in 2020 and is expected to reach USD 13.4 billion by 2025 (Source: [5])

**Competitor Analysis**
---------------------

* On Amazon, competitors offering Indian-inspired fashion products include:
 + Amazon Fashion's own brand, Amazon Essentials, offers a range of affordable fashion products with an Indian touch
 + Brands like FabAlley and Aha! by Taneira offer trendy and affordable fashion products with an Indian twist
 + Luxury brands like Sabyasachi and Anamika Khanna offer high-end Indian-inspired fashion products
* On eBay, competitors include:
 + Indian e-commerce platforms like Jabong and Myntra, which offer a range of Indian-inspired fashion products
 + Global brands like H&M and Zara, which offer trendy and affordable fashion products with an Indian touch
* On Etsy, competitors include:
 + Independent sellers offering handmade and unique Indian-inspired fashion products
 + Brands like Kriti by Shreya and Srishti by Pooja, which offer handmade and eco-friendly fashion products

**Pricing Strategy**
-------------------

* Our analysis suggests that the average price of Indian-inspired fashion products on Amazon, eBay, and Etsy ranges from USD 10 to USD 50
* Luxury brands like Sabyasachi and Anamika Khanna offer high-end Indian-inspired fashion products priced between USD 100 and USD 500
* Affordable brands like FabAlley and Aha! by Taneira offer trendy and affordable fashion products priced between USD 10 and USD 20

**Opportunities**
----------------

* The growing demand for sustainable and eco-friendly products presents an opportunity for our brand to offer environmentally friendly fashion products
* The Indian market holds immense potential, with a large and affluent customer base
* The popularity of online platforms like Amazon, eBay, and Etsy provides a convenient and accessible way to reach a global audience

**Recommendations**
-------------------

* Focus on offering unique and sustainable fashion products with an Indian twist
* Target the Indian market, with a focus on affluent and middle-class consumers
* Utilize online platforms like Amazon, eBay, and Etsy to reach a global audience
