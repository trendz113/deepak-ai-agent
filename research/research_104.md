## Pricing Strategies
Competitors in the E-learning platform niche use various pricing strategies, including:

* **Tiered pricing**: Byju's and Unacademy offer tiered pricing, with different plans and prices for different courses.
* **Discounts and promotions**: Coursera and Udemy offer discounts and promotions to attract customers.
* **Value-based pricing**: UpGrad and Great Learning offer premium courses at a higher price, based on the perceived value of the course.
* **Competitive pricing**: IMS Proschool and e-Mindz offer competitive pricing, similar to other competitors in the market.
* **Free trials**: CareerLabs and Edureka offer free trials to attract customers.
