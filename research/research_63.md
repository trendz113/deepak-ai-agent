**Reliable Suppliers of Handmade Crafts in India**
=====================================================

### Overview

For the online business targeting the Indian market, we need to find reliable suppliers of handmade crafts. Our research focuses on sourcing suppliers that offer high-quality products at competitive pricing, with efficient shipping options.

### Top Suppliers of Handmade Crafts in India

We've identified the following top suppliers of handmade crafts in India:

* **Craftsvilla**: Craftsvilla is an e-commerce platform that connects buyers with artisans from across India. They offer a wide range of handmade crafts, including textiles, jewelry, and home decor items. [1]
* **Ebay India**: Ebay India is a popular online marketplace that features a variety of handmade crafts from Indian artisans. [2]
* **IndianHandicrafts**: IndianHandicrafts is a Delhi-based handicrafts manufacturer that supplies handmade crafts to customers worldwide. They specialize in products such as wooden handicrafts, metal handicrafts, and more. [3]
* **Dhatri**: Dhatri is a Bangalore-based company that specializes in handcrafted jewelry and home decor items. They use eco-friendly materials and offer unique designs. [4]
* **Kala Raksha**: Kala Raksha is a Jaipur-based organization that promotes traditional Indian crafts. They offer a range of handmade crafts, including textiles, jewelry, and metalwork. [5]
